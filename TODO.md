# TODO

Open tasks are tracked as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/crjeder/cq-design/issues)
(migration: [#326](https://github.com/crjeder/cq-design/issues/326)). Anything tied to a
specific change lives in that change's `openspec/changes/<name>/tasks.md`.

Rationale, investigation notes and strategy discussion live in `documentation/todo-*.md`,
linked from the issues. Closed investigations are in
[documentation/todo-case-history.md](documentation/todo-case-history.md).
Completed tasks are in [documentation/DONE.md](documentation/DONE.md).

## Related ideas
Surveyed projects, ratings and open ideas live in [documentation/related_projects.md](documentation/related_projects.md).
